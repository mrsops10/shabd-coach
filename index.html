About this prototype

Who it is for

Learners who understand some English but struggle to find the right word, at work, in interviews or in daily life. Hindi hints act as a safety net.

The learning loop

  1. Coach says the word.
  2. You explain it in your own words.
  3. You use it in a sentence.
  4. Missed words return later.

Where AI helps

  • Understands explanations in your own words, Hinglish included.
  • Coaches your sentence and suggests a better one.
  • Answers follow-up questions about the word.
  • Creates word sets on any topic.

What I would measure

  • Day-1 and day-7 recall per word.
  • Where users drop off in a round.
  • Hint and "close" rates by level.
  • Which topics people request most.
